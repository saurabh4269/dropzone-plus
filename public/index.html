  1. 01

    Ring sees the zone

    Frames are checked against the zone you drew. The answer is CLEAR, NOT CLEAR or UNKNOWN, timestamped, and it expires after 90 seconds.

  2. 02

    Alexa tells the household

    About 8 minutes out, Alexa says what is in the way and offers the one thing that helps.

  3. 03

    Devices help, with your yes

    The pet door goes come-in-only, yard lights come on at dusk so the camera can see, and the sprinkler waits. Each one restores itself after the drone has left.

  4. 04

    The carrier gets one bit

    At T−60 s the carrier sees HOLD with a reason code, or NO_ADVISORY. It never receives video. After the drop, a Ring frame becomes the proof-of-drop receipt.

Safety and privacy

It can ask the drone to wait.
It can never tell it to land.

If DropZone is down, slow or unsure, the carrier does exactly what it does today.

"advice": "HOLD","authorises_drop": false,"hold_seconds": 60

Advisory only

The carrier endpoint returns HOLD or NO_ADVISORY, and authorises_drop is always false. Holds are capped at 3. The aircraft's own detect-and-avoid stays in charge.

CLEARNOT CLEARUNKNOWN

UNKNOWN is first-class

Offline camera, encrypted feed, an error, a stale frame or no zone: the answer is UNKNOWN, never CLEAR.

persondogvehiclefacesplates

Labels only

No face matching, no tracking across locations, no plates. The carrier gets reason codes, never images.

Your yes first

The pet door refuses to change without your explicit yes, and restores itself after the drop or after 15 minutes.

Data minimisation

In the browser path, frames are analysed on the device and never leave it. The server keeps only the latest verdict, in memory.

Carrier-neutral

It watches your own yard, not any carrier. It works with any drone operator and monitors no Amazon service's availability or performance.

For delivery partners

One small, read-only contract.

Poll it before the drop. It can ask for a delay. It can never authorise a landing.

  • Two answers. HOLD with reason codes such as PERSON_IN_ZONE, or NO_ADVISORY.
  • Fails open to today. CLEAR and UNKNOWN both map to NO_ADVISORY.
  • Bounded. Every advisory carries observed_at and valid_until. Holds are capped.
  • No media. No images, no video, no identities cross the boundary.

What's real and what's demo

Real

  • Zone logic: CLEAR, NOT CLEAR, UNKNOWN, expiry and zone audit
  • Advisory API, GET /api/v1/advisory
  • Object detection in the browser (TensorFlow.js COCO-SSD)
  • Ring Partner API in live mode: discovery, history, snapshots, live view
  • Ring webhook HMAC verification

Demo

  • Ring webhook events
  • Replay yard, actors and scripted detections
  • Alexa+ add-on utterances, also exposed via MCP
  • Smart pet door, yard lights and sprinkler
  • Delivery partner linking (Amazon Prime Air, Wing, Zipline, Flytrex, Manna): no live integration, not affiliated
  • Drone carrier consuming the advisory

DropZone is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Ring, Amazon or any drone operator.
