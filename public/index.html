What you get

Your yard is ready before the drone launches.

  • Hear about it 8 minutes out

    Alexa names the problem while there is still time: Drone in 8 minutes, and there's a dog in the drop zone.

  • Fix it with one yes

    The pet door lets the dog in but not back out, yard lights come on at dusk, and the sprinkler skips its run. Each one goes back to normal after the drop.

  • The drone waits instead of giving up

    A kid runs through at the last minute and the carrier gets HOLD for 60 seconds, not an aborted trip and a ground reroute.

  • Catch a bad spot on day one

    The zone audit flags hazards in downwash range, such as a pool 4 ft from the drop point, and suggests a safer patch of lawn.

  • Know where the box landed

    A before and after Ring frame becomes your receipt: inside the zone, outside it, or next to the pool.

  1. 01 · Once

    Draw your drop zone

    Drag four corners on a live Ring snapshot and name the spot. Mark the pool or the patio edge, and the audit tells you if the zone is too close.

    • Save several spots, like delivery addresses
    • Accept a suggested safer zone in one tap
  2. 02 · 8 minutes out

    DropZone checks and Alexa asks

    Ring frames are scored against your zone: CLEAR, NOT CLEAR or UNKNOWN. Each answer is timestamped and expires after 90 seconds.

    • Labels only: person, dog, vehicle
    • No device moves until you say yes
  3. 03 · 60 seconds out

    The drone waits, or lands

    The carrier asks for an advisory and gets HOLD with a reason code, or nothing at all. After the drop, a Ring frame shows where the box ended up.

    • Never more than 3 holds per delivery
    • Devices restore themselves afterwards

Safety and privacy

It can ask the drone to wait. It can never tell it to land.

If DropZone is down, slow or unsure, the carrier does exactly what it does today. The aircraft's own detect and avoid system stays in charge.

  "advice": "HOLD",
  "authorises_drop": false,
  "reason_codes": ["PERSON_IN_ZONE"],
  "hold_seconds": 60,

}

One small contract for drone partners

The endpoint returns HOLD with reason codes, or NO_ADVISORY. authorises_drop is always false. CLEAR and UNKNOWN both map to NO_ADVISORY, every answer carries valid_until, and holds are capped at 3.

CLEARNOT CLEARUNKNOWN

Unsure means UNKNOWN

Offline camera, encrypted feed, an error, a frame older than 120 seconds or no zone drawn: the answer is UNKNOWN, never CLEAR.

persondogvehiclefacesplates

Labels, not identities

No face matching, no plates, no tracking across homes. The carrier gets reason codes, never images.

Your yes comes first

The pet door refuses to change without explicit owner consent. It restores itself after the drop, or after 15 minutes if no drop is reported.

Frames stay on your device

In the browser, frames are analysed locally with TensorFlow.js. The server keeps only the latest verdict, in memory.

Works with any carrier

It watches your own yard, not the operator. Any drone partner can poll the same advisory.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Still curious? The README on GitHub covers every endpoint and test.

Do I need a Ring camera to try it?

No. The app opens with a demo home that has a simulated Backyard Cam, so you can draw a zone, get an Alexa heads up and see a HOLD without any account. With a Ring Developer Playground token and the local server, it switches to your real camera.

Does the drone company see my camera?

No. The carrier receives HOLD with a reason code such as PERSON_IN_ZONE, or NO_ADVISORY. No images, video or identities ever cross that boundary.

Can DropZone block a delivery forever?

No. Each hold lasts about 60 seconds and there are at most 3 per delivery. After that the carrier decides on its own, as it does today.

What happens if my camera is offline or it is too dark?

The answer is UNKNOWN, which the carrier treats like no advisory at all. At dusk, DropZone can offer to switch on the yard lights so the camera can read the zone again.

Will my pet door or sprinkler change without asking?

Never. Every device change waits for your yes, and each device goes back to its normal mode after the drop, or after 15 minutes if no drop is reported.

Does it work with Prime Air, Wing or Zipline today?

Not yet. Partner linking in the app is a labelled preview with no live integration, and DropZone is not affiliated with any operator. The advisory is a small, open contract any operator could poll.

Where is my video analysed?

In your browser, with TensorFlow.js running a COCO SSD model. If you run the server with AWS credentials, you can opt in to Amazon Rekognition instead. Either way the output is labels like person or dog, never faces.

Which camera works best?

Any Ring camera that can see the drop spot. A front doorbell covers driveway drops, and a backyard camera covers lawn drops.

What does it cost?

Nothing. DropZone is open source under the MIT licence. Clone it, run npm start, and it works in replay mode with no token.
